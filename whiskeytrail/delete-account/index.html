Delete Your Account

Sausalito Whiskey Trail, developed by Austin Hunter

Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Sausalito Whiskey Trail and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm twice — Delete My Account, then Yes, Delete Everything.

Your account and all of its data are deleted immediately.

No longer have the app?

Email ahunter916@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Sausalito Whiskey Trail account”, sent from the email address you signed in with (your Google or Apple account email). Deletion requests are completed within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirmed by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile (name, email, country)
  • All tasting logs, notes, and uploaded photos
  • Your trail progress, completions, and reward claims

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. No account data is retained after deletion — there is no additional retention period. You can also delete individual tastings at any time in the app without deleting your account.
